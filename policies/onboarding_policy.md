# Employee Onboarding Policy Handbook (synthetic)

All content below is synthetic sample data written for this capstone project.
It describes no real organisation, employee, or agreement.

## POL-001 — Probation period

Every new hire serves a probation period of 90 days counted from the official
start date recorded in the offer. The hiring manager files a written probation
review before the period ends. A probation extension of at most 30 days is
allowed once, and only with written HR approval. Confirmation of employment is
never automatic: the probation review must be closed explicitly.

## POL-002 — Mandatory security training in week 1

Mandatory information security and data-handling training must be completed
during the first calendar week of employment, before any production system
access is granted. The training covers phishing awareness, password hygiene,
and the classification of confidential records. An employee who has not passed
the security training keeps read-only sandbox access only.

## POL-003 — Equipment allocation by role

Equipment is allocated by role, not by request. Engineering and data roles
receive a developer laptop with 32 GB of memory and an external monitor.
Business, finance, and administrative roles receive a standard laptop with
16 GB of memory. Any hardware beyond the role baseline needs a documented
business justification approved by the department head.

## POL-004 — Account provisioning needs manager and HR approval

No system account, mailbox, or directory identity is created until both the
hiring manager and HR have approved the provisioning request. The approval pair
is recorded in the onboarding case file with a timestamp. Provisioning requests
that carry only one approval are rejected and returned to the requester; single
approval is never sufficient, regardless of urgency.

## POL-005 — Buddy assignment for the first month

Each new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy from the same team for the first
month. The buddy holds a short check-in during week one, week two, and week
four, and flags any blocker to the hiring manager. The buddy role is advisory
and carries no approval authority over the new hire's work.
